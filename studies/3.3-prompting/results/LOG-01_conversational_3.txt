## Root Cause Analysis

### Causal Explanation
The anomaly is caused by **mod_jk connector misconfiguration or communication failure** between Apache HTTP Server and backend application servers (likely Tomcat). The mod_jk module is repeatedly failing to establish or maintain worker connections, cycling through initialization attempts that succeed at the workerEnv level but fail at the worker communication level.

### Confidence Score: 0.85

- **E22, E23**: High occurrence of error states 6 (42 times) and 7 (18 times) specifically for mod_jk child workerEnv processes indicate persistent worker communication failures

- **E19, E20, E21**: Recurring sources all point to mod_jk initialization components

**Secondary Indicators:**
