# Summary of Operational Log Data

## Apache mod_jk Errors (Primary Issue)

**Error Pattern:** Recurring `mod_jk child workerEnv in error state` failures
- **Error States:** States 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 observed
- **Most Common:** Error state 6 (hundreds of occurrences)
- **Frequency:** Continuous failures from December 4, 2005 through July 27, 2005
- **Timing:** Errors occur 2-4 seconds after `workerEnv.init() ok` messages
- **Configuration File:** `/etc/httpd/conf/workers2.properties`

**Additional mod_jk Issues:**
- Multiple instances of `mod_jk child init 1 -2` errors
- Several `jk2_init() Can't find child [PID] in scoreboard` errors (children 1566, 1567, 2082, 2085-2087, 5053-5054, 5619-5622)
